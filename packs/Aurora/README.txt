# Aurora

Aurora is an API 1 pack: bright, natural and vivid. A deep blue sky with white volumetric clouds, strong sunlight with soft shadows and light shafts through the leaves, foliage that glows against the sun, ambient occlusion, clear water that mirrors the world and sparkles in the sun, warm lanterns at night and a vivid ACES tone curve.

Requirements: Prisma 1.0 or later and Luanti 5.17. Pick a profile (Potato, Low, Medium, High, Ultra) under Quality; the look options are separate.

The pack format and GLSL API are described in docs/shaderpacks.md, included in every Prisma release; Aurora's design, in docs/dev/AURORA.md of the prisma-shaders repository. Check the passes with prisma_check Aurora.
